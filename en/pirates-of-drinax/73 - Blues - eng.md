# Session: Repairs, Rumors, and Sindalian Relics

**PCs present:** Den, Pika, Klaiton, Lyra, Daijiro, Yaang-ki, Jimmy.
**NPCs present/mentioned:** Sal Dancet, Krrsh, Mari Jagad, King Oleb XVI, Thao Poloc.

**Main campaign developments:**
The crew has realized that random piracy is not profitable after the heavy losses suffered. In their effort to restore the Kingdom of Drinax, they have managed to secure a Sindalian academic expert in Torpol who possesses a message globe sealed since the final days of Noricum. This object could contain vital secrets and requires Drinax technology to be opened. Furthermore, they have begun to delegate missions, sending Jimmy to Goertel to track down a legendary Sindalian ship.

**Loose ends:**
*   What are the true intentions of the Third Imperium fleet that is evaluating ports like Blue?
*   Where exactly is the ship *Águila* (Eagle) in Goertel, and why have an Aslan clan (and Mari Jagad) put a price on its head or are searching for it?
*   What is being built in the secret shipyards under the ice on the slaver planet of Oghma?
*   What information is contained in the message globe sent by the Duke of Albe just before the fall of Noricum?
*   What happened to the traveler's daughter, kidnapped by a stealth ship that resembles Imperial or Aslan ships?

**Session joke:**
The best joke came when the crew was debating the danger of infiltrating the slaver planet of Oghma. Upon learning that the warlords specifically look for slaves who know how to read to work in their secret shipyards, the players joked that they would have to dress as Andalusian peasants and pretend to be illiterate "bumpkins" so they wouldn't be kidnapped for being engineers.
