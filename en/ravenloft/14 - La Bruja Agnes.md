# Session Title: Old Agnes's Cargo and the Closed Gates

**PCs Present:**
*   Ceres (played by Paloma)
*   Renoir (played by Nacho)
*   Erdan (played by Marcos)
*   Hulio (played by Emilio)
*   *(Note: Pulgar and Broseph are traveling in the cart, but their players are not active / they are unconscious in the fiction).*

**NPCs Present:**
*   Vistani Leader from the Tser Falls camp
*   Old Agnes (Witch)
*   The kidnapped child
*   Vallaki Guards

**Main Developments and Loose Ends:**
*   **Main Developments:** The group reached level 5 and reviewed their Tarokka card readings (revealing clues about the windmill, a golem, beast skins, and the land of wine). During their night journey toward Vallaki, they intercepted a street vendor, Old Agnes, discovering that she was carrying a kidnapped child in her cart. After a tough combat where the witch destroyed the group's wagon and a brain-shaped monster (Grell) ambushed them, they managed to rescue the child. However, they arrived too late at Vallaki and found the gates closed by order of the Burgomaster.
*   **Loose Ends:** 
    *   Old Agnes escaped alive and vanished into the mist; they know she operates at the hallucinogenic cookie windmill, so she will surely seek revenge.
    *   They have a drugged 6-year-old child under their care and need to find out who he is and how to cure him.
    *   The group's cart is wrecked (doorless and with its roof burned) and they are trapped outside the walls of Vallaki in the middle of the night.
    *   They have two "magical/hallucinogenic cookies" from the witch whose exact effect they do not know.

**Session Joke:**
*   **"Erdan's Alarm Clock":** When the witch magically puts the entire group to sleep except for Erdan (Marcos), he discovers that the only way to wake them up is by inflicting damage on them. With complete coldness, Erdan decides to use the *Magic Missile* spell, firing a force dart at each of his companions (Ceres, Hulio, and Renoir), dealing real damage just to force them out of bed and into combat.

### Scene 1: Farewell at the Vistani Camp
**PCs Present:** Ceres, Renoir, Erdan, Hulio.
**NPCs Present:** 
*   Vistani Leader (Young, handsome, observant, with a reserved and elegant attitude, drinks wine from a superior goblet. Archetype: Enigmatic Leader / Mysterious Merchant).
*   Vistani Gypsies (Pranksters, partygoers. Archetype: Picturesque Locals).

**Location(s):** Gypsy camp north of the Tser Falls, Barovia.
**Loot Obtained:** None.
**Scene Summary:** 
After the card reading by Madame Eva, the characters spend time with the Vistani. Renoir fits in perfectly playing music and telling jokes, playing hard to get in the face of a woman's advances. Hulio drinks and enjoys himself, while Erdan remains distant and aloof, earning the mockery of the gypsies. Ceres, living up to her sense of responsibility, stays sober and pressures the group to leave before night falls. The Vistani leader watches them from a distance and invites them to return in the future when they have gold to trade for magic items brought from outside the mist.

---

### Scene 2: Crossing Paths with the Dessert Cart
**PCs Present:** Ceres, Renoir, Erdan, Hulio.
**NPCs Present:** 
*   Old Agnes (Woman in her late 50s, long gray hair, many wrinkles, missing half her teeth, unsettling smile. Archetype: Disguised Witch / Sinister Vendor).

**Location(s):** Winding road between Barovia and Vallaki, near the fork toward the windmill.
**Loot Obtained:** None (yet).
**Scene Summary:**
Traveling in a hurry in their wagon under intermittent rain, the group crosses paths with an old woman pulling a large cart covered with a tarp. She introduces herself as Old Agnes, a vendor of exclusive cookies made at the local windmill. Renoir and Ceres decline her offer, arguing that they have no gold, which infuriates the old woman. However, thanks to their high perception, Renoir and Ceres notice that beneath Agnes's tarp there is a sack moving with what appears to be a small creature inside, instantly connecting this with Renoir's prophecy about the crying children at the windmill.

---

### Scene 3: Fire, Tentacles, and Friendly Missiles
**PCs Present:** Ceres, Renoir, Erdan, Hulio.
**NPCs Present:** 
*   Old Agnes transforming (Grows in size, elongated jaw and nose, blooming violet hair, exposed sagging breasts, grayish skin hard as steel, sharp claws. Archetype: Revealed Monster / Night Hag).
*   Grell / Flying brain (Levitating creature, it is a brain with a chicken's beak and multiple tentacles ending in a sharp claw. Archetype: Ambushing Beast).

**Location(s):** The winding road in the middle of the night.
**Loot Obtained:** 
*   A child about 6 or 7 years old, semi-conscious/drugged.
*   Two of the witch's cookies.

**Scene Summary:**
Upon being discovered, Agnes throws a powder that puts almost the entire group to sleep. Erdan, the only one awake, casts magic missiles at his own companions to wake them through pain. Finding herself cornered, the old woman reveals her true and grotesque demonic form, unleashing a cone of lightning that shatters the doors and sets fire to the roof of the group's wagon. A chaotic combat breaks out: Ceres uses silence and healing magic, Hulio is weakened by a curse from the witch but manages to strike her, and Renoir stabs her with his silver rapier while invisible. 

The witch flees magically, but suddenly a Grell falls from the sky, ambushing the group. The monster paralyzes and grapples Hulio with a tentacle. After a hard exchange of blows where Erdan drains life from the tentacle, Renoir pierces the brain, and Ceres scorches it with a *Sacred Flame*, Hulio manages to break free and finish off the beast. Ceres rescues the drugged child from the witch's cart. In the distance, through the mist, they see the witch watching them with spite before vanishing completely.

---

### Scene 4: The Closed Gates
**PCs Present:** Ceres, Renoir, Erdan, Hulio.
**NPCs Present:** 
*   Vallaki Guards (Smiling from the safety of the wall, unyielding, mocking. Archetype: Stubborn Bureaucrats).

**Location(s):** Outer wall gates of Vallaki.
**Loot Obtained:** None.
**Scene Summary:**
The group manages to put out the fire in their wagon, leaving it completely wrecked. They arrive at the gates of Vallaki in the middle of the night, but the guards atop the wall deny them entry, citing a decree from the Burgomaster that forbids opening the gates after nightfall. Renoir attempts to persuade them using the child rescued from the witch as an argument, but the guards laugh at the story, believing it to be a children's fairy tale, and assume the child is simply drunk. Ceres considers using mind-control magic (*Command*) to force them to open, but decides against it upon realizing that the guards will know they have been enchanted, which would make them fugitives in the city. The group is left stranded outside, having to decide how to survive the night.
