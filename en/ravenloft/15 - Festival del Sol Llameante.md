**Major developments and loose ends:**
*   **Major developments:** The group discovers that parents are selling their children to the mill witches in exchange for pastries (or to pay off debts) and decides to adopt the rescued boy, naming him Yuri. They manage to kidnap Irina from the burgomaster's house, killing a guard in the process, and hide her in the church. However, the next day, the man with the reptile arm hires one of the mill witches as a diviner. She finds Irina, resulting in Father Lucian's arrest and the girl being returned to the mansion.
*   **Loose ends:** 
    *   What will happen to Father Lucian and the altar boys who have been tied to posts in the public square?
    *   Renoir left a signed note taking the blame for Irina's kidnapping in the church. Will it fall into the hands of the guard?
    *   Hulio killed a guard with a battleaxe, a weapon he visibly carries on his belt. Will they link him to the crime?
    *   What will the characters do now with the boy Yuri who is under their care?

**Session Joke:**
The best joke of the session occurred during Irina's tense rescue. Hulio, a fighter with 18 Strength, decides to carry Irina to escape through the window. The DM sets a very high difficulty (20) to climb down the facade, citing her weight. Emilio (Hulio) gets comically outraged, questioning the girl's "Constitution" and complaining that a full-fledged warrior shouldn't have trouble slinging a 1.60-meter-tall noblewoman over his shoulder, sparking laughter across the group as they imagined Irina as a "wardrobe" or a "muscle woman."

### Scene 1: The Parents' Rejection
*   **PCs present:** Renoir, Ceres.
*   **NPCs present:** Vallaki guards (tired, uninterested - *Archetype: Inept guard*), Yuri's parents (expressionless, cold, under the influence of substances - *Archetype: Corrupted citizens*), Yuri (semi-conscious, tearful - *Archetype: Innocent victim*).
*   **Place(s):** Streets of Vallaki at night / Parents' house.
*   **Any loot obtained:** None.
*   **Summary of the scene:** The characters, accompanied by the guard, return the boy they rescued from the witches to his home. Renoir convinces the guards of his version of events. Upon knocking on the door, the parents (who seem to be under the influence of the mill's pastries) reject the boy, stating that they handed him over to the mill to pay off a debt and want no trouble with the ladies. They slam the door in the guards' faces, who leave the boy lying on the ground and walk away. The group decides to take care of the child.

### Scene 2: Assault on the Burgomaster's Mansion
*   **PCs present:** Renoir, Hulio, Ceres, Pulgar.
*   **NPCs present:** Irina (slender noble, under the effects of a strong charm/drug, docile, giggly - *Archetype: Altered damsel*), Mansion guard (scared, noisy - *Archetype: Cannon fodder*).
*   **Place(s):** Exterior, roof, and attic of the Burgomaster's house.
*   **Any loot obtained:** None.
*   **Summary of the scene:** The group infiltrates the burgomaster's house to rescue Irina before the wedding. After several comical stumbles while trying to climb, they manage to reach the attic window. Ceres casts a Silence spell on the area. Hulio forces the window open and runs into an alerted guard. Without hesitation, Hulio hacks the guard to pieces in absolute silence. They find Irina heavily drugged and happy. They forcibly gag her because she will not stop laughing and trying to talk, and Hulio carries her on his back to escape by descending a tree.

### Scene 3: Sacred Refuge
*   **PCs present:** Renoir, Hulio, Ceres.
*   **NPCs present:** Father Lucian Petrovich (devout, scared, worried about retaliation - *Archetype: Reluctant ally*).
*   **Place(s):** Stables of the Blue Water Inn, St. Andral's Church.
*   **Any loot obtained:** None.
*   **Summary of the scene:** After ruling out hiding Irina in the inn's stables due to the racket made by the ravens, Renoir uses an Invisibility spell to take the girl (holding hands) to the church. He wakes Father Lucian and convinces him to hide Irina in the hideout (the crypt) where they previously kept a reliquary. The priest reluctantly accepts upon seeing the girl's condition. Renoir leaves a note next to her explaining their motives so that, upon waking from the drugs, she does not panic.

### Scene 4: The Guard and the Ravens
*   **PCs present:** Renoir, Ceres, Hulio.
*   **NPCs present:** Man with reptile arm/Sergeant (tall, burly, intimidating, with a monstrous reptile arm - *Archetype: Villain's enforcer*), Guards, Martikov Family (scared innkeepers - *Archetype: Oppressed citizens*).
*   **Place(s):** Blue Water Inn (Main hall and stables).
*   **Any loot obtained:** None.
*   **Summary of the scene:** The next morning, the man with the reptile arm barges into the inn with his guards looking for Irina and his man's killer. While they inspect the place, Ceres and Renoir go to the stables. To prevent a guard from discovering the loft (where Strahd's journal is hidden), Ceres uses Animal Handling and Renoir emits a caw to agitate the ravens. A swarm attacks the guard, causing him to fall and break a leg. Ceres uses her medical skills to treat him, earning a grateful look and causing the guard to give up searching the loft.

### Scene 5: Festive Morning and Rumors
*   **PCs present:** Renoir, Ceres, Hulio.
*   **NPCs present:** Blisky (peculiar toymaker, helpful - *Archetype: Eccentric artisan*), Wolf hunters (rugged, pragmatic - *Archetype: Expert locals*).
*   **Place(s):** Vallaki's main square, Toy shop, Inn.
*   **Any loot obtained:** Key information about the environment and NPCs.
*   **Summary of the scene:** During the gray and rainy "Festival of the Blazing Sun," the PCs split up to gather information. Renoir delivers a brilliant performance in the square, discovering that the guard has hired the fortune-teller Morjana (the mill witch) to find Irina. Hulio talks to local hunters about the mysterious "chupacabra" that hunts humans and about survival tactics. Ceres inquires about the pastry sellers, discovering that their cookies provide the inhabitants with a false and deep happiness. Renoir also visits Blisky to pay him in advance for some marionettes. Finally, they decide to name the rescued boy "Yuri," who tearfully tells them how his parents handed him over to the witch.

### Scene 6: The Witch's Triumph
*   **PCs present:** Renoir, Ceres, Hulio.
*   **NPCs present:** Morjana/mill witch (old, with gray hair, malicious - *Archetype: Vengeful villain*), Man with reptile arm, Father Lucian, Irina.
*   **Place(s):** Vallaki's main square.
*   **Any loot obtained:** None.
*   **Summary of the scene:** The group hears a commotion and heads to the square. There they see the guard, led by the man with the reptile arm and guided by the witch Morjana, pulling Irina (now more sober and struggling) out of the church to return her to the mansion. Father Lucian and his assistants are arrested and tied to posts in the square. As she walks away triumphant, the witch places her glass eye into her empty socket, looks directly at the adventuring group, and smiles at them, showing that she knows perfectly well who they are and what they have done. The group decides not to intervene, accepting their defeat for the moment.
